# Case studies

Three product groups measured on BACI HS12 release V202601, 2012 to 2024, with Tunisia as the importer. The
release holds 142,112,452 flows, 717,531 of them into Tunisia, across 5,084 imported products and 200 supplier
countries. No row was rejected.

Values are nominal current US dollars. For a chapter or a heading, supplier values are summed over every product in
the group before shares and the HHI are computed, so the group reads as one market. Each table can be reproduced in
the app at `#/product/10`, `#/product/1001`, `#/product/31` and `#/product/85`, or from
`GET /api/products/{code}/history`.

The HHI runs from 0 to 1. The app flags 0.25 and above as highly concentrated.
